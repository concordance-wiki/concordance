# Default French stopwords of the `fr` language pack: one word per line, lowercase.
#
# A stopword is a word that names nothing on its own in any corpus: articles,
# prepositions, pronouns, conjunctions, the auxiliaries and the light verbs in
# every form, the adverbs and connectors of prose, quantifiers, numerals, and the
# few nouns that only count or place ("chose", "fois", "manière"). A word that could
# be a business term somewhere ("contrat", "règle", "source", "partie", "cours",
# "type", "donnée", "mise", "prise", "vue") or that ends a technical compound
# ("point d'entrée", "fin de vie") never goes here: a project adds it to a
# file listed under `inference.stopwords`.
#
# A title or alias that is a stopword never enters the recognition dictionary, and
# the keyword discovery drops every n-gram starting or ending with one. Stopwords
# are compared on the same form as the texts: accents stripped, plurals reduced,
# so "faites" and "faite" are one entry to the engine. One section per class,
# alphabetical inside a section. Elided forms (l', d', qu') are listed without their
# apostrophe, as the tokeniser cuts a word at its apostrophe; so is "aujourd'hui".
# Project files listed under `inference.stopwords` are added on top of this list.
