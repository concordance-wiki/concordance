The template of a page follows the lead of its group of twin resources: a markdown note keeps the template of its type and gets its documents folded under the article, each behind the line "Also available: name.docx · Text document · 24 pages" (`entity.alsoAvailable`, `DocumentView.summary`), the viewer, the extracted text and the download on demand; a document without a note, or a note of the `document` type, gets the document page; a transcript the meeting page. Every template then says the same way, at the foot of its properties block, which files the build merged into the page: "3 files grouped — same base name" (`entity.groupedFiles`, the criterion worded through `entity.groupedBy.*`), each file with its kind, and "Separate these files" leading to `project.contribute_url` or to the lock section of the configuration guide (`EntityPageProps.grouping`, `GroupedFiles`); the meeting page keeps the clues of the reconciliation under it and the document page drops its "Same document" block (`document.sameDocument`, `document.groupedNote`, `document.roleOriginal`, `document.roleNotes`, `meeting.files`, `meeting.filesGrouped`, `DocumentPageView.files`, `MeetingProps.grouping` removed; `MeetingProps.groupingNote` and `document.kind.markdown` added). New gallery state `entity-page-with-twin`; the realistic corpus gains the Word twin of a rule.
