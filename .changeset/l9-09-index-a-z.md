A–Z index: the page opens on the sentence counting the words used in the documentation and those with a written page, folds its filters (by type, by space, without a definition) behind a button whose values link to the results page, keeps every letter in the bar with the inactive ones struck through and counted, and lists the words of every letter under a heading with its count in a table of four columns, the word, its type, the first line of its page and the number of pages citing it; a word without a definition is dotted, marked "no definition" and described by the passage of the file that uses it most, quoted with the title of that file, as the note under the table explains. The `Index` slot gains the letter, the type label and the summary of every entry, the counts, the filters and the labels of the page; the catalogues gain the `index.*` messages.
