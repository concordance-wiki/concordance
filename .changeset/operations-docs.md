Schemas: every property of `config.schema.json`, `theme.schema.json`, `profile.schema.json` and `lock.schema.json` carries a `description`, and the documented defaults are declared with `default`, so that the reference pages under `docs/reference/` are generated from the schemas; the profile schema shares its attribute and label definitions through `$defs`.
