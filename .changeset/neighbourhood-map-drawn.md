Neighbourhood map: the map is drawn for every page with a neighbour, the total the model holds worded under it when it exceeds the six nodes drawn, the pointer to the mentions panel kept for a page without any neighbour; the fold line counts the pages the map draws and opens on a small network mark; the map takes the frame of the reference design, the page at its centre in the accent, small grey neighbours, the labels on a halo of the frame colour, the side column widening while the fold is open, every row of the list opening on the accent mark with the type as plain text; a keyword page draws its map from its co-occurrences, six nodes at most, the pages as full nodes and the other noteless words as dashed ones, and the block of accompanying words goes with its messages `keyword.companions` and `keyword.noCompanion`.
