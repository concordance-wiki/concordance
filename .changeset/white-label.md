White-label theme: `loadTheme` reads and validates `theme.yaml` (name, inline SVG logo, favicon, palettes, fonts, radius, footer with an optional credit replacing `mention_tool`, project stylesheet in the `project` layer, assets folder), a mode switch island remembers the reader's colour scheme, and the gallery renders the project's or a plugin's theme file.
