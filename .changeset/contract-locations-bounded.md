A contract path that leaves its source (absolute, or climbing above the root) is refused with a `W-CONTRACT-UNREACHABLE` whose message names no path of the machine, and is never copied under the output; a contract URL pointing at the build machine, its link-local neighbours or a private network is never fetched. `contractPathIn` and `refusedContractUrl` are exported by the core package.
