Chrome of the default theme: one type family for the text, Instrument Sans, and IBM Plex Mono for paths and identifiers, both shipped under `assets/fonts/` with their licence and bound by `@font-face` rules in `site.css`, never fetched from a font host; a warm light palette and a dark palette measured on its own, the theme palette gaining the optional `label`, `soft` and `highlight` colours (`theme.schema.json`, derived from `muted`, `bg` and `border` when absent) and the contrast checker reading every text colour over the page, a surface and the soft surface, with a test pinning the measured ratios of both palettes; the top bar with the mark and the name, the search field showing its `/` shortcut, the links to the spaces, the A–Z index and the recent changes and the mode switch, the to-do link and its count moved to the footer; the entity page laid out in three regions, the tree of the space of the page on the left (`spaceOf`, at most forty pages of a folder listed around the current one), the breadcrumb, the title, the line naming the type, the last change and the space, the note and the path of the file with its edit link in the centre, and on the right the properties, the table of contents and the related pages, the neighbourhood folded behind its line; the mentions panel becoming the related pages, one entry per citing page with its type, its passage count and an excerpt marked "cited" when the page writes a link, ordered by number of passages, with a text filter and a type filter once the island runs; three widths, three columns from 1180 px, the tree folded from 768 px, one column under it, every block a closed `<details>` the stylesheet keeps in view where the layout has room; the `entity-page-corporate` gallery state on a rule of the fixtures corpus; the `related.*`, `nav.index`, `site.spaces`, `site.recent` and `entity.*` messages of the chrome in both catalogues, `mentions.explicit` and `mentions.inferred` retired.
