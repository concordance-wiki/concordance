Neighbourhood map in the panel: the fold of the entity page and the keyword page, once open, shows the map as the panel draws it, a back control with the title of the map and the name of the page in its summary, the other blocks of the panel hidden from the desktop width; the `Neighbourhood` slot gains the distance the model records, a type filter of checkboxes that hides nodes, edges and rows from the stylesheet alone, the framed map with its legend, the head of the list and the note on why the map stops at six; its labels come from the site language through the `neighbourhood.*` messages; `EntityPageProps.mapOpen` serves the fold open and the gallery gains the `entity-page-map` state.
