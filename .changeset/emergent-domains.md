Emergent domains proposed from the neighbourhood: `inference.domains` names the terms with enough neighbours as pivots and reports every unclassified note within the radius as `I-DOMAIN-SUGGESTED`, listed in the build log, assigned only under `assign`; the lock file promotes a proposal under `domains`, and every entity records the origin of its domain under `domain_origin`.
