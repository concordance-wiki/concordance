# Claims kickoff

Participant-1 presented the claim journey: declaration at the branch, review by the claims handler, indemnity net of the deductible. Participant-4 asked whether the waiting period applies to a claim declared in the first month of a cover; Participant-2 answered that the waiting period is defined per cover in the policy schedule and is not checked by the Claims API today.

Participant-2 proposed to extend the claim deadline to ten working days: see [claim deadline extended](../decisions/claim-deadline-extended.md). Participant-1 will document the waiting period in the claims framing.
