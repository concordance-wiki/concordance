# Caps review

Participant-3 showed that the annual cap lets a member pay a whole year in one free payment, which the branches dislike. Participant-1 proposed a monthly cap of one twelfth, checked in the Payments API like the annual cap: see [monthly cap introduced](../decisions/monthly-cap-introduced.md).

The screen Free payment entry and the portal's Online payment display the returned message without change.
