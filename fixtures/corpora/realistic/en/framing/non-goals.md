# Non-goals

What the first version does not do: no build summary posted to the forge, no incremental rebuild, no live editing, no hosted service, no binary served from the site.

The [Canonical model API](../specs/api/canonical-model.md) is the only export of a build; the [to-do page](../specs/screens/to-do-page.md) is the only place where a finding is acknowledged.
