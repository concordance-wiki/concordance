# Payments framing

Framing note for the payments scope. It lists the free payment and scheduled payment cases, the premium call, the annual cap and the nightly settlement, and leaves the payments made outside any contract out of scope for the first release.

The [Payments API](../specs/api/payments.md) is the only entry point for a payment; the [PAYMENT table](../specs/tables/payment.table.md) is written by the nightly settlement only.
