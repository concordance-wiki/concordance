# Portal vision

The member portal gives members a view of their contracts and a way to pay online, then to declare and track a claim, then to terminate. Every business rule stays in the back-office APIs: the portal is a channel, never a second implementation.

This note declares no application on purpose: the portal is described by the [portal review](../meetings/2026-04-02-portal-review.md) and its decisions.
