# Claims framing

Framing note for the claims scope: declaration at the branch, review by the claims handler with an assessor, indemnity net of the deductible, reserve updated every working day. The waiting period of each cover is applied by hand for now.

The [Claims API](../specs/api/claims.md) is a legacy SOAP service; the [claim review](../specs/screens/claim-review.md) screen works on the claim file directly.
