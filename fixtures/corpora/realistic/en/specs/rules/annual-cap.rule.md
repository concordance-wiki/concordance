# Annual cap

The total of payments on a contract over a calendar year may not exceed the cap set at subscription. The check happens in the [Payments API](../api/payments.md) only, as recorded in [cap checked server-side](../../decisions/cap-checked-server-side.md).

## Applies to

- [Free payment entry](../screens/free-payment-entry.md)
- [Online payment](../screens/portal/online-payment.md)
- [Payments API](../api/payments.md)
- [Payment](../objects/payment.md)
