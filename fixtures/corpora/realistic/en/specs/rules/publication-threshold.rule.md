# Publication threshold

A candidate expression gets a keyword page when it occurs at least three times across at least two files. The count happens when the [Canonical model API](../api/canonical-model.md) is written, as recorded in [threshold applied in model](../../decisions/threshold-applied-in-model.md).

## Applies to

- [Keyword page](../screens/keyword-page.md)
- [Search results](../screens/search-results.md)
- [Canonical model API](../api/canonical-model.md)
- [Keyword page](../objects/keyword-page.md)
