# Section heading match

A section heading is matched to the profile without regard to case or accents, so that a heading typed without its accent still maps to its relation. A heading that differs by a word is prose.

## Applies to

- [Mentions panel](../screens/mentions-panel.md)
- [Entity page](../screens/entity-page.md)
- [Link](../objects/link.md)
