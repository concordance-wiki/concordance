# Monthly cap

The free payments on a contract over a calendar month may not exceed one twelfth of the annual cap. Introduced by [monthly cap introduced](../../decisions/monthly-cap-introduced.md); the [annual cap](annual-cap.rule.md) still applies.

## Applies to

- [Free payment entry](../screens/free-payment-entry.md)
- [Online payment](../screens/portal/online-payment.md)
- [Payments API](../api/payments.md)
