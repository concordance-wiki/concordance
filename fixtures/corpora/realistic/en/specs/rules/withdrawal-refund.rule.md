# Withdrawal refund

When the member withdraws during the cooling-off period, every payment received on the contract is refunded at the next nightly settlement, without deduction.

## Applies to

- [Termination request](../screens/termination-request.md)
- [Terminate a contract](../processes/terminate-a-contract.md)
- [Refund](../objects/refund.md)
