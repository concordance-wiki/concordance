# Stale after 180 days

A note untouched for 180 days is stale: the [staleness report](../batches/staleness-report.md) lists it and the entity page shows a badge. The glossary gets a longer threshold, since a term changes less often than a screen.

## Applies to

- [To-do page](../screens/to-do-page.md)
- [Entity page](../screens/entity-page.md)
