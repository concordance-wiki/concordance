# Subscribe a contract

A prospect accepts a quote and becomes a member.

## Steps

1. The [account manager](../roles/account-manager.md) creates the member on [member file](../screens/member-file.md).
   - If the [member age limit](../rules/member-age-limit.rule.md) is not met, go to step 5.
2. The account manager creates the contract from the quote on [contract overview](../screens/contract-overview.md).
3. The member signs the direct debit mandate and the general conditions.
4. The contract takes effect at its effective date.
5. End.
